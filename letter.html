A letter to my love 💌

♡   FOR ABDULLAH   ♡

My Abdullah,

Happy birthday to the love of my life. ❤️

We've been together since 2022, and when I think about how far we've come, my heart feels so full. You are not just someone I love; you're the person I want beside me through the ordinary days, the exciting days, the difficult days, and all the beautiful days we haven't lived yet.

I wish I could put into words how much you mean to me. I hope this new year of your life brings you peace, happiness, good health, success, and so many reasons to smile. I hope you always remember how deeply you are loved, how important you are to me, and how proud I am to be part of your life.

And soon, Inshallah, we will begin a new chapter together. I can't wait for the little everyday things—talking about our day, laughing at silly things, supporting each other's dreams, and making a home full of warmth and love. I pray Allah puts barakah in our future and keeps our hearts kind toward each other.

Thank you for being my Abdullah. Thank you for being part of my story. If life is a book, you are one of my most beautiful chapters—and I hope we get to write so many more together.

Happy birthday, my favourite human. Today, tomorrow, and in all the years to come, my heart is cheering for you.

Forever yours,
Your Laru ♡
